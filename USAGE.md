# ALCWARE Design System Usage

## Read Order

1. `DESIGN.md` — canonical meaning, hierarchy and rules.
2. `tokens.css` — exact compiled values; do not substitute or derive alternate brand values.
3. `components.html` — executable visual proof of ALCWARE components and interaction patterns.
4. `preview/` — focused human previews for colors, typography, spacing and components.

## Design Highlights

ALCWARE is a compact operational UI system. DM Sans is the intended typeface. Primary 600 `#00909E` is the main CTA/accent. Primary 900 `#004F58` is a deep brand shade, not the default button. Neutral and White are structurally separate from Primary. Components use pill primary/secondary/danger buttons, 12px controls, 20px cards and 10px table actions.

## Do

Use `components.html` as the visual authority for component geometry. Preserve the exact Primary, Neutral, Semantic and Auxiliary families. Keep tables compact and make the primary entity visually stronger than metadata. Keep secondary actions quieter than primary actions. Use Tabler icons.

## Avoid

Do not generate a generic SaaS component kit. Do not substitute Inter as the intended brand font. Do not make Primary 900 the default CTA. Do not reinterpret White or Neutral as Primary. Do not add dashed button families, oversized marketing controls, heavy shadows or arbitrary gradients.
